These are hypothetical shares of positive annual operating value. Avoided bills are not necessarily distributable cash. Actual benefits, including for renters, require ownership and transfer agreements; these outputs do not establish cash available or household access.

Environmental accounting

Unknown impacts remain unknown. Any gross displacement proxy covers assumed on-site use only; it is not a net climate result.
